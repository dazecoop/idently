Summary (250 characters max):

Sign in to one site as many accounts at once, each in its own tab. Sessions name themselves from your login, with colours, notes, search and grouping by site. A notch shows which account you're in. Free and local-only.

Description:

Idently is for anyone who signs in to the same site as more than one account: clients, test users, admin and personal. Each login lives in its own Firefox container, so you can be signed in as all of them at once, side by side.

It's built for lots of logins. Fifteen client accounts on one dashboard become fifteen sessions, each named after the email it signs in with. Type part of a name and press Enter to be back in any of them.

- Every account, its own tab: Click "New session tab" on any site, sign in, and the session is one click away from then on. Sessions are grouped by site, and their tabs are filed into a tab group.

- Always know which account you're in: A notch at the top of the page shows the session's name and colour. Click to copy the name, or drag it out of the way.

- Sessions that name themselves: A site rule reads the account from the sign-in form and names the session as you type. The rule builder finds the field for you.

- Search and organise: Search names, sites and notes together. Edit any session's name, notes and colour, and merge duplicate groups. Expired logins are flagged.

- Backup and recovery: Export sessions to a file, with or without their logins, and recover containers after a reinstall.

- Works alongside Multi-Account Containers: Idently isn't a replacement; it's a more in-depth take on the same idea for when you have many logins. Multi-Account Containers doesn't name containers for you, and its single list gets hard to manage past a handful. You can use both at once, but it isn't required.

- Private and local: No network requests, no account, no tracking. Sessions stay in Firefox on your computer.

Free and open source under the MIT licence. Containers aren't available in private windows.
